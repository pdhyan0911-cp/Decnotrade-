MARKET BASICS

Understand what
you're looking at.

Charts are a way to visualise how price has moved over time. They help organise information, but they cannot predict the future with certainty.

THE LEARNING LIBRARY

Make market language
feel less complicated.

Look up a term, get a plain-language definition, and keep building your foundations.

C

Candlestick

A chart symbol showing open, high, low and close for a selected period.

T

Trend

A broad direction inferred from a sequence of price swings over time.

V

Volume

A measure of trading activity, defined by the market and data provider.

R

Risk

The uncertainty of outcomes, including the possibility of losing money.

T

Timeframe

The interval represented by each candle or data point on a chart.

M

Trading range

A period when price moves within a broad zone instead of a clear direction.

No matching terms. Try a shorter search, such as “risk” or “trend”.

GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you begin.

What Decrotrade does—and what it doesn't.

Is Decrotrade suitable for beginners?+

Yes. The lessons start with basic chart vocabulary and explain concepts in plain language. Learn at your own pace and revisit topics as needed.

Does this website show live prices or trading signals?+

No. The chart is illustrative, not connected to live market feeds. Decrotrade does not provide trade signals or execute trades.

Can I make trades or deposit money here?+

No. This website is for education only. It does not accept deposits, connect to a broker, or support real-money or demo trading.

Does Decrotrade guarantee profits?+

No. Markets are uncertain. Educational material cannot guarantee results and is not personal financial advice.
