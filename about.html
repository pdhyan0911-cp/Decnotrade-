OUR PURPOSE

Learn with clarity.
Practise with care.

Decnotrade is an independent educational project designed to make market terminology, chart concepts, risk arithmetic and reflective practice easier to explore. It combines short learning material with a virtual workspace so visitors can understand the ideas before deciding what they want to study next.

What you can do here

  • Read beginner-friendly explanations of market and chart vocabulary.
  • Explore illustrative instruments and learn how to read price displays.
  • Use a virtual portfolio to understand how order quantity and account balance interact.
  • Try educational calculators and record the reasoning behind a practice decision.
  • Track lessons and challenges locally in the browser.

What the website does not do

Decnotrade does not provide live exchange prices, personal investment recommendations, broker services, real-money orders, account registration, cloud-synced profiles or a live AI adviser in this version. Prices, chart movements and portfolio results are sample simulations intended to explain interface behaviour. A simulated result is not evidence that a strategy will work in real markets.

Our learning approach

We focus on understanding terms, checking assumptions and asking what could go wrong—not on promising profits or predicting what a market will do. Calculators use simplified inputs and should be treated as arithmetic demonstrations. Real financial decisions involve uncertainty, costs, personal circumstances and risks that this site cannot assess.

Privacy and transparency

Practice activity, learning progress, profile preferences and journal entries are saved in the browser on the device being used. They are not synchronised to an online account. Avoid entering passwords, account numbers, precise location or other sensitive details. See the privacy notice for more information and the educational disclaimer for the site's limitations.
